# HUNT3D brand system

## Positioning
Evidence-first AI security research workspace for bug bounty hunters and pentesters. The brand blends underground security culture with disciplined, credible research tooling.

## Core idea
**Find the signal.**

## Primary lockup
Angular H signal mark inside a broken targeting reticle, paired with a heavy HUNT3D wordmark. The acid-lime numeral **3** is the signature interruption in the wordmark.

## Logo variants
- `assets/hunt3d-mark.svg`: primary signal mark on void black.
- `assets/hunt3d-horizontal.svg`: horizontal wordmark and tagline for headers and banners.
- `assets/hunt3d-stacked.svg`: centered lockup for title cards and square social layouts.
- `assets/hunt3d-icon.svg`: lime app tile for favicon, launcher and avatar use.
- `assets/hunt3d-light.svg`: dark logo for light backgrounds.

## Palette
- **Acid** `#C5FF00`: signature highlight and active states.
- **Void** `#050706`: primary background.
- **Bone** `#F5F7F2`: primary text and light surfaces.
- **Graphite** `#171C18`: panels and borders.
- **Signal red** `#FF465A`: dangerous findings and destructive states only.

## Typography
Use a condensed, heavy grotesk for headlines and wordmarks, a clean sans for interface copy, and monospace for terminal output, IDs, evidence and metadata. Suggested fallback stacks: Arial/Helvetica/sans-serif and ui-monospace/SFMono-Regular/Consolas/monospace.

## Visual language
Broken reticles, angular cuts, precise grids, evidence panels, restrained scanlines and terminal annotations. Use texture in campaign art, not behind dense product UI. At small sizes, use the app icon variant.

## Voice
Direct, technical, evidence-led. Confident without claiming autonomous exploitation or guaranteed findings.

Preferred copy: **Hunt the bug. Prove the impact.** / **Find the signal.** / **Research with evidence.**

Avoid “hack anything”, guaranteed vulnerabilities, fake terminal output presented as real product activity, or claims that the product replaces researcher judgment.

## Clear space and usage
- Keep clear space around the mark equal to at least one quarter of the symbol width.
- Do not stretch, rotate, or casually recolor the core logo.
- Use the light variant on pale surfaces and the acid variants on void backgrounds.
- The app icon is for square contexts; do not put its tile behind the horizontal lockup.
- Keep a minimum 4.5:1 contrast ratio for normal interface text.
