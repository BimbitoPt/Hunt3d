# Hunt3d brand system

## Positioning
Evidence-first AI security research workspace for bug bounty hunters and pentesters. The brand sits between underground security culture and serious research tooling.

## Core idea
**Find the signal.** Campaign line: **Hunt the bug. Prove the impact.**

## Personality
- Curious, technical, direct, a little irreverent.
- Underground energy, professional evidence.
- Confident but never makes unsupported security claims.

## Colors
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Void | `#0B0D0C` | Main background |
| Acid | `#C5FF35` | Signature accent, focus and positive states |
| Bone | `#F5F7F2` | Primary text and light surfaces |
| Alert | `#FF4D5A` | Real risk, failure and alert states only |
| Panel | `#121512` | Cards and surfaces |
| Line | `#30372E` | Borders and dividers |

## Typography
- Display: heavy/condensed sans, tight tracking, short uppercase headlines.
- Technical: monospace for metadata, paths, evidence labels and terminal-style details.
- Body: neutral, highly readable sans-serif.
- Prefer real bundled fonts in production; use system fallbacks until licensed font choices are finalized.

## Logo
Canonical SVG: `brand/assets/hunt3d-mark.svg`. Concept combines angular H/claw geometry and a targeting reticle. Pair with a heavy HUNT3D wordmark. Use lime on dark or dark on lime; maintain clear space equal to the reticle radius. Do not stretch, add bevels, or apply permanent glitch effects. This is a concept mark, not trademark-cleared.

## Visual language
Use terminal details, target markers, evidence IDs, scan lines, sharp geometry, restrained noise and deliberate negative space. Keep product UI clean. Reserve distortion for transitions and campaign graphics.

## Voice
Short, specific, evidence-led. Good: “From lead to reproducible finding.” Avoid “fully autonomous hacking”, guaranteed findings, or presenting roadmap features as shipped.

## Accessibility
Maintain strong text contrast. Do not rely on lime/red alone to convey state; include text/icon labels. Motion should respect reduced-motion settings where applicable.
