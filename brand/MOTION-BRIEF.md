# Hunt3d — Remotion motion brief

## Deliverable
40-second brand/product promo. Master: 1920×1080, 30 fps, 1200 frames. Deliver H.264 MP4 with embedded mixed audio and a still thumbnail. Make a vertical cutdown only after the master is approved.

## Creative idea
Underground metalcore teaser meets a real security research workbench. The energy belongs to bug bounty hunters and pentesters: terminal culture, late-night focus, technical humor, clean evidence. Never use generic Matrix rain or hooded-hacker clichés.

## Audio
Use an original or properly licensed instrumental metalcore track. Downtuned guitars, tight drums, glitch percussion, terminal clicks and impact hits. Edit to musical transients. Embed the final audio in the render; do not ship a silent preview or rely on external playback. Check mix on laptop and mobile speakers.

## Animation vocabulary
- Kinetic typography with short overshoots and decisive settling.
- Reticle sweeps, scan-line wipes, UI cards that snap into grid.
- Glitch used as punctuation, not as a constant overlay.
- Drum hits drive cuts; guitar changes drive scene transitions.
- Keep key UI text legible for at least 1–1.5 seconds.
- Respect reduced-motion in any web version; promotional video can remain energetic without strobing.

## Sound design layers
1. Licensed/original music bed.
2. Short, restrained terminal keyboard and UI confirmation sounds.
3. Whooshes and low impacts for scene changes.
4. Final logo impact and clean tail.

## Brand tokens
Void `#0B0D0C`, Acid `#C5FF35`, Bone `#F5F7F2`, Alert `#FF4D5A`. Use `brand/assets/hunt3d-mark.svg` as the source mark.

## Product-truth rule
Do not portray roadmap ideas as available product features. Use actual app captures where possible. If the app is still a concept/prototype, label illustrative screens as concepts. Do not imply authorization bypass, guaranteed vulnerability discovery, or unattended exploitation.
