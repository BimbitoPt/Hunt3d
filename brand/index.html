03 / VOICE

Talk like a hunter.
Back it with proof.

Short, specific, a little irreverent. Security people respect receipts more than hype. Keep claims tied to what the product actually does today.

MESSAGING PILLARS

Signal over noise

Help researchers focus on meaningful leads, not another pile of activity.

Evidence over theater

Reproducible steps, clear impact, reviewable artifacts.

Human-led research

AI assists the workflow. Researchers stay accountable for scope and decisions.

04 / MOTION

40 seconds.
All signal.

Metalcore energy, terminal realism, kinetic type and a little hacker-culture humor. The music drives the edit; the evidence earns the ending.

MOTION RULES
  • 30 fps; 1920 × 1080 master
  • Hard cuts and impact frames on drum hits
  • Reticle sweeps and scan-line wipes between scenes
  • Typography can slam, jitter, then lock perfectly still
  • Keep UI text readable long enough to register
  • End with a clean logo lockup and one memorable line
SOUND DIRECTION

DOWNTUNED.
NO FAKE HYPE.

Original or properly licensed instrumental metalcore: downtuned guitar, punchy drums, glitch percussion, terminal clicks and cinematic impacts. Embed the music in the exported MP4 and mix for mobile speakers.

LICENSE MUSIC BEFORE RELEASE
